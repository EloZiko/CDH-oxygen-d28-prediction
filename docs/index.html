Research tool · Congenital diaphragmatic hernia

Oxygen dependency at day 28

Prenatal prediction for isolated left-sided CDH, from four routine markers.

Research use only. This is not a medical device. The model must not replace clinical judgement.

  • For singleton pregnancies with isolated left-sided CDH, diagnosed before birth.
  • The prediction assumes survival to day 28. It is not a risk of death.
  • It reflects current management, including FETO when indicated. Do not use it to decide on FETO.

Prenatal markers

Leave a marker empty if it was not measured. At least two of the four are needed.

%

Observed-to-expected lung-to-head ratio (ultrasound).

Liver herniation

Liver herniated into the thorax (ultrasound).

mm

Deepest vertical pocket of amniotic fluid (ultrasound).

%

Observed-to-expected total fetal lung volume (MRI).

The model gives a class, not a probability: its probabilities are not calibrated (article, Appendix G).

    About the model

    Algorithm
    CatBoost (gradient-boosted trees), 30 trees of depth 2
    Development
    50 fetuses, Paris-Saclay (France), 2012–2025: AUC 0.878 (± 0.076)
    External validation
    98 fetuses, Leuven (Belgium): AUC 0.827 (95% CI 0.734–0.908)

    Internal AUC from nested cross-validation (mean ± SD).
