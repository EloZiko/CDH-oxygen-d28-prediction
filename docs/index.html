    Abstract

    Objective. To develop and externally validate a machine-learning model predicting, from prenatal data, oxygen dependency at day 28 of life, a recognised surrogate for chronic respiratory outcome, in fetuses with isolated left-sided congenital diaphragmatic hernia (CDH) who survive to that age.

    Methods. In this retrospective study, a development cohort of 50 fetuses with isolated left-sided CDH alive at day 28 (Paris-Saclay, France, 2012–2025) was used to train six classifiers (logistic regression, random forest, XGBoost, HistGradientBoosting, CatBoost, TabPFN), with scaling, imputation and hyperparameters jointly tuned by nested cross-validation. Four predictors were fixed a priori on clinical grounds: observed-to-expected lung-to-head ratio (o/e LHR), liver herniation, deepest amniotic fluid pocket, and observed-to-expected total fetal lung volume (o/e TFLV). Models were externally validated on an independent cohort of 98 fetuses with isolated left-sided CDH from Leuven, Belgium.

    Results. CatBoost without imputation performed best, with an internal AUC of 0.878 (± 0.076) and an external AUC of 0.827 (95% CI 0.734–0.908), ahead of TabPFN (0.861/0.782) and random forest (0.845/0.820). Simple imputation strategies (no imputation or most-frequent value) performed at least as well as multivariate ones. Liver herniation was the main contributor to predictions (mean SHAP of 0.144) followed by o/e LHR (0.045). Recursive feature elimination lowered performance compared with the a priori clinical feature set.

    Conclusion. A machine-learning model based on four routine prenatal markers predicted day-28 oxygen dependency in isolated left-sided CDH, with good discrimination in an external cohort. Once its calibration has been improved and validated in a larger cohort, it could serve as a bedside tool during prenatal counselling.

    The model

    The tool runs the final model of the article, exported from Python. It gives exactly the same results.

    Algorithm
    CatBoost (gradient-boosted trees), 30 trees of depth 2, balanced class weights
    Predictors
    o/e LHR (%), liver herniation (yes/no), deepest amniotic fluid pocket (mm), o/e TFLV (%). At least two of the four are needed.
    Missing markers
    No imputation: CatBoost treats them as lower than any measured value
    Output
    High or low risk of oxygen dependency at day 28. No probability is shown: the predicted probabilities are not calibrated (article, Appendix G).
    Explanation
    SHAP values, computed exactly for each fetus
    Privacy
    The prediction is computed in your browser: the values you enter are never sent anywhere.
